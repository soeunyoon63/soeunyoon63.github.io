Overview

Revenue.io is an AI-powered B2B platform that helps sales teams drive predictable performance through conversation data and real-time insights.
As the company expanded, the need to reposition Revenue.io as a category-defining platform has grown. I supported the rebrand and built a visual system that can scale across marketing, product, and internal comms.
* full brand guidelines available on request

Impact

I focused on refinement and structure in the work, introducing true 3D elements alongside 3D-inspired graphics paired with layered gradients to create depth and dimensionality, and giving the brand a dynamic presence. By building a modular system rather than isolated assets, the brand scales seamlessly across the website, marketing campaigns, decks, and event materials.
